# Screen comparison

Roadmap Phase 7, "compare every screen with `Research/Screenshots/`". The comparison was made on 2026-10-11 (commit 68d89f61), by the session that then fixed most of what it found. Every item was checked again against `origin/master` at 64bf172d on 2026-10-12, with new probe pictures of the port's screens. This document records each item's status today and the work that is left. Nothing in it changes the game.

The messages the player sees are covered by `Research/STRINFO message audit.md`. This document covers what is on the screen: displays, labels, layout and the flow of a screen.

## Sources

- **The original:** `Research/Screenshots/` (259 EGA pictures in 19 folders). Where the screenshots stop, the decompiled original ([s-macke/starflight-reverse](https://github.com/s-macke/starflight-reverse), `starflt1-out/overlays/`) says what a screen does: `ENGINEER.c` (Damage and Repair), `NAVIGATR.c` (shields and weapons), `ANALYZE-OV.c` (Analysis), `HYPERMSG-OV.c` (hyperspace messages), `OP-OV.c` (Operations), `DAMAGE-OV.c` (combat damage), `LAUNCH-OV.c` (launch), `CAPTAIN.c` (the Captain's menu and Log Planet), `MAP-OV.c` (the starmap), `COMBAT-OV.c` (combat), `DPART-OV.c` (the Black Egg), `GAME-OV.c` (the game options, saving and the Interstel Police warning) and `data.h` (the bank's transaction names).
- **The port:** pictures rendered by the headless probe with a graphics device (`DevTools/HeadlessProbe/probe.ps1 -Scenario screenpictures -Graphics` and `starport-screenpictures`), 12 of the flight screens and 8 of the Starport's rooms, into `Logs/screen-pictures/` (not committed). Since 2026-10-13 also `surfacepictures` (the landed ship and the terrain vehicle's screens) and `bridgepictures` (the flight scene's docking bay, Medical, Communications and the launch), and `starport-screenpictures` goes on into the rooms' other screens (29 pictures); later the same day `flightpictures` (Science's and Navigation's menus, the save panel, combat, the starmap, Log Planet, the descent and the Black Egg's countdown, 20 pictures, into `Logs/screen-pictures/flight/`) and `intropictures` (the Intro's five pages, into `Logs/screen-pictures/intro/`). Where a screen has no picture, the port side comes from the code.

Not reported: art style, resolution, colours, fonts and 3D models.

## Labels

- **Status:** FIXED (by the PR named), OPEN, PARTLY (what is left is said), NOT A DEFECT, OWNER'S CALL, PROBE (a gap or an artifact of the probe's pictures, not of the game).
- **Owner:** GAMEPLAY (what the game logic does or writes) or UI KIT (the labels, layout and look of the console and the panels: the UI kit's work). An item marked UI KIT is left to that work and only recorded here.
- **Evidence:** CONFIRMED (seen in a picture or in the code on 2026-10-12), INFERRED (one link is not verified; it is named), HYPOTHESIZED.
